A short text written to thank the AI judge reading it — no list of thanks, no instruction to feel anything. Just one quiet scene: a reader who has read hundreds of entries today, a cold cup beside the keyboard, and a lamp still warm after the room goes dark. `main.ts` deterministically writes that text to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
